Our research develops integrated circuits that operate from millimeter-wave through sub-terahertz frequencies. We study mixed-signal and analog/RF circuit techniques for transmitters, receivers, frequency generation, amplifiers, switches, and other critical front-end building blocks in CMOS technologies.

## Research directions

- Analog/RF, millimeter-wave, and sub-terahertz CMOS circuit design
- Transmitter, receiver, frequency-generation, and power-amplifier building blocks
- De-embedding and calibration methods for on-chip measurements
- Active and passive device characterization at very high frequencies
- Circuit and device techniques that improve output power, efficiency, bandwidth, and reliability

This work builds on Korkut Kaan Tokgöz's doctoral research on millimeter-wave CMOS transceivers for 100 Gb/s wireless communication and continues toward practical sub-terahertz integrated systems.

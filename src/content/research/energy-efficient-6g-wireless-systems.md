Future wireless networks require very wide bandwidths while operating within strict energy and hardware constraints. We investigate millimeter-wave and sub-terahertz communication systems that combine ultra-high data rates with energy and spectral efficiency.

## Research directions

- Ultra-high-data-rate wireless transceivers and links
- Energy- and spectrum-efficient transmitter architectures
- Millimeter-wave and sub-terahertz phase-locked loops for beyond-5G and 6G systems
- Circuit-to-system co-design, link budgets, and hardware-aware communication models
- Sub-terahertz communication and sensing architectures

The group's work connects integrated-circuit design with system-level performance. Current research includes millimeter-wave and sub-terahertz PLLs for beyond-5G/6G systems, along with new communication technologies in the millimeter-wave and terahertz bands.

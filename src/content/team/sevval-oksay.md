She was born in Istanbul, Türkiye, in 1997, and graduated from Saint Benoît French High School in Istanbul in 2016. She received the B.S. and M.S. degrees in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from Özyeğin University, Istanbul, Türkiye, in 2021 and 2026, respectively. Her M.S. thesis focused on the design of a dual circularly polarized horn antenna and feed network for full Ka-band satellite communications (SATCOM).

She is currently pursuing the Ph.D. degree in Electronics Engineering at Sabancı University, Istanbul, Türkiye, and is a Graduate Student Member of IEEE. Her research interests include electromagnetics, antennas, RF/Microwave circuits, and integrated circuit technologies for advanced communication systems.

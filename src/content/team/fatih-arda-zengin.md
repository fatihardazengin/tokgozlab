Fatih Arda Zengin is an M.Sc. candidate in Manufacturing Engineering at Sabancı University with a background in Industrial Engineering and research interests at the intersection of artificial intelligence, embedded systems, energy technologies, and advanced manufacturing.

His research focuses on developing machine learning and deep learning methods for engineering systems, particularly battery health monitoring and remaining useful life (RUL) prediction. He is interested in translating AI models from research environments into resource-constrained embedded platforms using FPGA acceleration, model quantization, and edge AI techniques for real-time applications.

His research experience also includes physics-guided artificial intelligence and machine learning for semiconductor device modeling, including GaN transistor design and optimization. His broader interests span intelligent energy systems, battery management systems, embedded AI, FPGA-based machine learning, semiconductor technologies, and data-driven manufacturing.

Through his work, he aims to bridge machine learning, hardware-aware AI, and engineering physics to develop efficient, reliable, and deployable intelligent systems for next-generation energy and manufacturing applications.

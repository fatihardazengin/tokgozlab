Saman Mokhtabadamri is an Electronic Engineering researcher specializing in RF, mm-wave, and sub-THz CMOS integrated-circuit design. He started his PhD in 2023 at Sabancı University, Türkiye, and is pursuing a dual-degree PhD with National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University (NYCU), Taiwan. His research focuses on high-frequency transceivers and phase-locked loops (PLLs) for ultra-broadband wireless communication systems.

His research explores advanced CMOS circuit techniques for mm-wave and sub-THz applications, with particular interests in RF integrated circuits, frequency generation, wireless communication systems, and semiconductor technologies for next-generation communications. He holds an M.Sc. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering and has published research on injection-locked frequency dividers, CMOS quadrature oscillators, and PLL architectures.

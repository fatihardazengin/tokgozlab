I'm currently a Master's student in the Electronics Engineering program at Sabancı University. I have also done my Bachelor's degree on Electronics Engineering here at Sabancı. My research centers on digital integrated circuit development, ranging from RISC-V processor architectures and SoC design, FPGA implementation, hardware accelerators for machine learning and TinyML applications, and digital ASIC design.

On my first year, I have worked on an Edge-ML project funded by Tübitak that calculated the Remaining Useful Life (RUL) of batteries without using Cycle Index (CI) data. The project featured RNNs such as LSTM, SimpleRNN and GRU. Currently working on a runtime and gate-configurable flexible Edge-ML hardware project.

My longer-term interests lie in aerospace and sensor-related digital systems, with a particular passion for RISC-V and computer architecture.

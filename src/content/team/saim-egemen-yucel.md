I am a graduate researcher pursuing an M.Sc. in Materials Science and Nanoengineering at Sabancı University, holding a B.Sc. in Physics Engineering from Ankara University. My background combines computational modeling and hands-on laboratory work: I have used density functional theory (DFT) to investigate electronic and structural properties of 2D materials, while gaining practical experience in cleanroom fabrication and materials characterization techniques. Aiming for an academic career in device physics and emerging technologies, I am currently focusing my research on the design and experimental realization of quantum materials with potential applications in Edge AI hardware.
